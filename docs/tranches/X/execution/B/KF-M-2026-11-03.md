## KF-M2: the cure
- **Attitude.** The stored truth is the container-local attitude Q (`TransformState.quaternion`, in f64). The world attitude is G·Q.
- **Drag.** One finger runs Shoemake's anchored arcball, q(a,b) = [a·b, a×b] with R = 2h. It is built in the screen frame and conjugated into Q: Q' = G⁻¹·q·G·Q₀. A new `attitudeQuat()` single-sources G.
- **Pan.** Pan is in screen px, rendered as `translate3d(G⁻¹t)`.
- **Two fingers.** Two fingers are one similarity:
  - zoom k = |d|/|d₀|;
  - twist = atan2(d₀×d, d₀·d);
  - pan is anchored on the centroid;
  - each channel has a soft dead zone (4°, 0.04 in log k, 6 px). It is paid back as the gesture grows, so nothing jumps.
  - The centre is clamped to V.
- **One multitouch source.** `useOrbitalPinch.ts`, every touch* listener and `useOrbitalPointer.ts` are deleted. `useOrbitalGesture.ts` is now the one Pointer Events reader. gesture* events only call preventDefault while fingers are down. With no finger down they drive the desktop-Safari trackpad (s = s₀·scale).
- **Coast.** The coast is rotation-only:
  - ω is measured per unit time over an 80 ms attitude ring. The KFA-82 hold rule is kept.
  - ω₀ = min(0.5·|ω|, 2π).
  - k = 9/s, set through the kept `inertiaFactor` mapping (default e^(−9/60)).
  - It uses the closed-form `decay()` sampled at elapsed time, so it is identical at any frame rate.
  - It is 0 under reduced motion.
  - The `*0.8` damp, the EMA and `perTargetFrame` are deleted.
- **Readout.** The readout is Y·X·Z with branch and pole continuity and unwrap, folded at rest. The echo check compares rotations (|dot| < 1−1e-12).
- **Rendering.** A version tick replaces the `void rotate.x/y/z` dependency. There is one model write and one emit per frame.
- **Unit gates (vitest):**
  - **G2.1:** 1000 random closed paths return within < 1e-12 rad; after 1e6 composes, ||q|−1| < 1e-12.
  - **G2.2:** no matrix step reaches 10× the median. A pure pitch through the pole has no readout step over 2°. A yaw loop to 720° is monotone. The 1e4 round-trip holds |dot| ≥ 1−1e-12.
  - **G2.3:** pure pinch k = 2 ± 1e-9; twist 90°; pan 1:1.
  - **G2.4:** gesture* writes nothing with fingers down; one write per frame; trackpad scale ± 1e-6.
  - **G2.5:** a flick coasts 0.56, 0.46 and 0.35× its own gesture; |ω| falls below 1% of ω₀ in ≤ 520 ms; 30, 60 and 120 Hz agree within < 1%; a held release coasts 0; twist ω is the same for 2 events or 8; PRM gives 0.
  - **G2.6, G2.7** and the axis latch.

## KF-M1: the cure
- **V.** The mobile `.stage-cell` content box is now V:
  - block-end padding: `calc(var(--transport-band) + var(--stage-sheet-reserve-block, 0px))`;
  - inline-end padding: `calc(var(--page-gutter) + var(--stage-sheet-reserve-inline, 0px))`;
  - `container: stage / size`.
- **The reserve.** The pane publishes the sheet's settled PEEK size and its OPEN lift (`useStageReserve`). These come from the sheet's own detent geometry (glass's computed min-block-size floor and the consumer's max), once per rung commit and never mid-drag. The reserve is the same at every rung, so the stage rect stays still (J.WZ).
- **The OPEN glide.** It runs on the compositor, on the new `.stage-glide` wrapper: translate −lift/2, and scale to fit the region above the sheet. It rides `--spring-dock` and is `none` under PRM.
- **Cube size.** The cube is `min(18rem, min(100cqi,100cqb)/2.2)`.
- **Home.**
  - The hero-recede lift is deleted; it counted the band twice.
  - The start-screen band ends at `--transport-band`.
  - The landscape column gap is `--space-body`.
- **Gutter.** The gutter is spelled once. The App.vue `.scene-host` copy is removed, and the page-gutter test is re-pinned on the stage cell.
- **PEEK.** The portrait PEEK lands on a row boundary (`rowBoundaryPeek`, ≥ glass's floor). The cube's PEEK becomes 0.238.
- **Roster.** The roster `live-session-mobile` CLOSED predicate now reads the rung below OPEN, because PEEK is measured rather than a fixed 0.12. The rest of that predicate is unchanged.

## Deviations (recorded, not hidden)
- **Dead zones are paid back.** They are a soft dead zone, eff = raw − thr²/raw past the threshold, rather than a plain subtraction. A plain subtraction cannot meet the G2.3 1:1 gates (k = 2, 90°, 100 px).
- **Pole band.** The pole band is |r12| > 1 − 1e-12, not 0.9999. Holding Z is only an exact spelling of q at the pole itself, and the 1e-12 round-trip gate needs that.
- **G2.6 in the browser.** It is gated on the arcball's surface rate (θ·h per px, which should be 1). The front-face-centre displacement reads 30 px for a 40 px drag because the Matrix channel's authored pose tilts every face off the view axis by cos β. The pure gate (frontal face 40 ± 6) is green.
- **Reserve source.** The reserve is read from the sheet's own computed geometry at each rung commit. There is no ResizeObserver on its border box: a ResizeObserver would fire on every frame of the spring back to PEEK.
- **Home cube side.** `--home-cube-side` stays a viewport-unit token bounded by V's band (now `--transport-band`). It is not in cq units, because the start screen's rows live outside the stage container and must read the same token.
