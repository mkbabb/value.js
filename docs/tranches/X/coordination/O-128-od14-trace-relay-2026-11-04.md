1. FRAME RATE IS NOT THE DEFECT ON A GPU. On the GPU path, local and prod, desktop and mobile, every transition gives:
- rAF p50 8.3 ms, p95 8.7–17 ms, max mostly 17–26 ms (one 41.7 ms in a collapse, one 50.9 ms in a menu open, one 58.4 ms in a mobile face swap);
- 0–3 dropped 60 Hz frames;
- one 60 ms long task, in mobile face-to-actionbar r1;
- one LoAF over 50 ms, in desktop menu-open r1 (60 ms, 7.6 ms of it style/layout).
For example, desktop collapse ran 92 rAF frames over 840 ms with 1 dropped; expand ran 109 over 935 ms with 0 dropped; face-to-actionbar ran 95 over 838 ms with 1 dropped.
On the SoftwareRenderer path, desktop drops 7–34 frames per transition with 50–75 ms LoAFs. Those LoAFs carry about 0 ms of script and under 6 ms of style/layout. The trace puts the cost on the GPU-process VizCompositorThread (SoftwareRenderer::DoDrawQuad, 905 of 1046 ms), while CrRendererMain is about 13 % busy. So software compositing of the backdrop-filtered 3600x2000 surface accounts for those drops, not dock main-thread work.

(b) COLLAPSE/EXPAND IS A NON-UNIFORM ROOT SCALE. The root's scale goes from 1 to 0.124 with a Y of 1, while border-radius stays 9999px. So the pill caps squash into ellipses mid-morph, and the 1.5px border and the backdrop blur are scaled in x with them. The children are counter-scaled by 1/s.
At settle, the radius switches in one frame from the scaled pill to the collapsed rest radius "50% / calc(0% + 16px)". That is a squared 16px trailing cap on the collapsed seal: the OD-5b cut-cap in a third posture.
Source:

- layers.css:93-105 and layers.css:128 (--dock-live, --dock-size-scale and the inline-size reserve);

(e) CAP RADII ARE SCROLL-DRIVEN border-radius ANIMATIONS on the backdrop-filtered plate. getAnimations() shows gl-dock-cap-inline-start and gl-dock-cap-inline-end on a ScrollTimeline, attached in every state (run.css:483-523, keyframes at run.css:557+). ANIMATING PROPERTIES SEEN BY getAnimations():
- the plate's border-*-radius (the ScrollTimeline caps);
- dock buttons: background-color, color and scale transitions (120–200 ms), plus ::before opacity and --specular-x/-y;
- the menu popover .glass-reveal: filter, opacity, scale and translate (200 ms), and glass-reveal-out [scale+opacity+filter] on exit;
- value.js's own: .color-field border-color (200 ms) and crown-appear (550 ms).
None of width, height, clip-path, transform or backdrop-filter shows up as a CSS animation. They move through the JS spring's inline custom properties (--dock-morph-t, and the per-face --dock-t), which getAnimations() cannot see and the compositor cannot run off the main thread.
