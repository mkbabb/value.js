### X.P.W1.d The honest bench

- **Goal**: a bench that cannot silently measure itself in the wrong state.
- **Mechanism**: Opus 5 seat (M-23 §2). Port `bench/bench.ts`, `aggregate.mjs`, and `finalize.mjs`,
  and restate the method with three structural changes, each traceable to a measured fact:
  1. **One fresh process per cell (PT-03).** `PACKRAT_ARMED` is a module-global one-way latch —
     `packrat-entry:678` false, `:722` true, read at `:682`/`:714`, with **no assignment back to
     false anywhere in the bundle**; it is set by `makeMemoized()`, and `resetPackrat()` clears the
     memo store without disarming. O-15 measured UNARMED **93.9** → ARMED **138.2** ns/parse
     (**1.47×**) with `resetPackrat()` leaving **139.3**. Therefore a harness that arms the latch in
     an early cell measures every later cell at the armed rate — *a property of the harness, not of
     the engine*. Each cell runs in its own process and asserts the latch false at entry and at exit.
  2. **Interleaved cells, median of scored rounds, printed sink** — the parser-band's G7 method
     (40 rounds, first 10 discarded, cells interleaved per round), which is what made the
