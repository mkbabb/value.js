## Why
- **The gap.** value.js parses CSS through five BBNF modules compiled at runtime by `@mkbabb/bbnf-lang` 0.1.4 onto parse-that 0.8.2. The bench of record (`bench/records/2026-09-23-x-p-w6-x.json`) reads it **1.11–2.79× slower** than the retired hand parser (`grammar.ts` at `2155142b`). The judge's fresh-process cells re-read stock at **1.34–2.42×** on the six value entries and **6.06×** on `parseStylesheet`.
- **Where the time goes** (survey-profile, route seats): `mapState`'s `Object.create(state)` (21% of self time, megamorphic state); error bookkeeping and `console.error` on every failure (F-b-2); `Parser.lazy` trampolines (376); an ASCII-only, flag-blind first-character dispatch (F-b-3, F-b-4); value.js's stylesheet tag protocol (36% of the sheet).
- **The route, chosen on re-measured numbers** (judge): *staged emission, ahead of time*. route-ts-compiler's flag-aware first-unit analysis and staged emitter (one JS function per rule and mode), delivered with route-aot-codegen's discipline (checked-in module, sha256 drift header, typed action manifest, frozen oracle). The judge's arm `jx-aot-pos` read **below 1.0 in 35/35 fresh-process cells** (worst .882; medians color .819 · scalar .726 · value .494 · values .502 · keyframe .695 · timing .790 · stylesheet .768), 9/9 rounds on all 7 entries in one joint process. Runtime bundle: no parse-that, no bbnf-lang.
- **Not landed**, on numbers: ts-compiler's closure backend (stylesheet median 1.052, 3/10 cells below 1.0); engine-fusion (stylesheet 1.174); parse-that-only cures on the stock interpreter (`np-direct`: stylesheet 2.56, color 1.64); aot-codegen's own emitter (stylesheet 1.040/1.057 in 2 cells; it inherits 0.1.4's dispatch, so it builds F-b-3/F-b-4 into its output). The Wasm/Rust DFA route cannot take value.js's 32 lookarounds and `wasm/pkg-node` panics.
- **What the corpus mix hides** (critic): on the **56 accepted** keyframe selectors (`from`, `to`, `50%`, `entry 0%`, `cover`, what keyframes.js sends), `jx-aot-pos` reads **1.32–1.38× slower**, 0/11 rounds below 1.0, in 4 runs; every arm is slower there (stock 2.13, aot-text 1.55). The whole-corpus 0.695 is carried by 29,888 rejected sources. The likely cause is a fixed per-call cost (~0.30 vs 0.21 µs: `ruleOf(grammar())`, the `RunResult`, the entry wrapper, `src/css/bbnf/index.ts:33`). No prototype cures it. Unit `.k` owns it.
