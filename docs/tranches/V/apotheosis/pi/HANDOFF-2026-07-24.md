## 8. Claims and findings the receiving audit must assay

### Architecture

- Direct static combinators are sufficient for the accepted number and current
  token-family candidates.
- A regex terminal is not itself a lexer; a second runtime/tape/object layer
  is the rejected element.
- Recursive combinator block/function productions are permitted and required;
  imperative balanced scanners and broad remainder capture are not.
- Production-family isomorphism is semantic ownership and import direction,
  not cosmetic one-file parity.
- The final stylesheet root must reach every claimed family or explicitly
  declare another entry root.

### CSS semantics

- Keyframe selector has four arms:
  `from | to | <percentage [0,100]> |
  <timeline-range-name> <percentage>`.
- The named-range percentage is mandatory and unbounded; the bare percentage
  is bounded; the seven names are
  `cover`, `contain`, `entry`, `exit`, `entry-crossing`, `exit-crossing`,
  and `scroll`.
- Comma-list composition belongs above the singular selector.
- Timeline-range semantics have one owner under values and are consumed by
  keyframes and properties.
- The exact R1–R12 counterexamples and W2/W3 audit findings remain candidate
  fixtures, but none grants acceptance to old code.

### Evidence and performance

- A self-asserted JSON receipt is not independent evidence.
- A holdout needs pre-author content custody to earn blindness.
- A benchmark must execute exact comparator implementations over the same
  operation and bytes; copied regexes, directional smoke, or missing raw
  attempts grant no win.
- Failure attempts remain in the record. No retry erases them.
- UTF-16 code-unit throughput is not UTF-8 MB/s.
- Exact hashes prove identity, not semantic adequacy.

### Process

- The tranche produced far more process artifacts than accepted parser code.
- Repeating a general gate generator for every terminal is forbidden by the
  owner’s direct-work and parsimony edicts.
- Use one small reusable evidence shape; feature-specific work belongs in
  grammar, fixtures, browser/spec witnesses, and comparable benches.
- Corpus-analysis automation is not the parser critical path.
