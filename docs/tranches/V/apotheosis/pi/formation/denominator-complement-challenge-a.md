safer than allowing an unproved class-level exclusion to contaminate every
later feature boundary.

label/credit boundary).** Of the 26 rows, ten have an explicit `[[!SPEC]]`

remove any row from review.

closure).** The list is a hand-authored disjoint map. The classifier performs
