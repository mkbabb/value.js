# `KF-SELECTOR` boundary challenge B — REJECT

**Seat:** independent architecture/compatibility skeptic, 2026-07-22.  
**Disposition:** REJECT the proposed feature row before candidate authoring.

## Total-tranche finding

The direct parse-that grammar is feasible without a lexer, atom layer, token
objects, CST, or source scanner. Published parse-that 1.0.0 has the required
terminal, alternation, sequencing, dispatch, state, repetition, and EOF
facilities. The proposed row nevertheless bundles unresolved dependencies and
cannot preserve the frozen compatibility seam as written.

## Wave/row finding

The row called its contract sealed while leaving result, serialization, and
diagnostics unresolved. It also mixed a grammar-only candidate hash with a
benchmark operation that included an undefined public adapter. Those are
different features and non-identical operations.

The present `KeyframeSelector` type has only four names and an optional offset.
That carrier flows through `KeyframeRule`, `KeyframesBlock`, `StylesheetItem`,
`Stylesheet`, `parseStylesheet`, and `collectKeyframes`. The compatibility
decision therefore affects a graph of types and doors, not only
`parseKeyframeSelector`.

## Feature findings

- Freeze the singular normative selector only after its direct terminal
  dependencies are accepted; put comma-list composition in its own row.
- Give the grammar candidate a candidate-local observation door. Give the
  compatibility adapter a later, separate benchmark and differential.
- Pin the exact historical source/tree and provenance graph. Because H and B
  independence is not established, a fourth clean-room candidate is mandatory
  unless an affirmative genealogy proof closes the issue.
- Freeze failure offset, expected category, original-source mapping, and
  non-string behavior at the correct grammar/public boundary.
- Do not cast new names through the old four-name type or silently lose them.
  Formation must explicitly choose an evolved compatible type, a versioned L4
  surface, or an acknowledged lossy legacy adapter that receives no full-L4
  credit.

No implementation bytes were reviewed or accepted.
