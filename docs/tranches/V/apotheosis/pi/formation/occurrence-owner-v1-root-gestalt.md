## Gestalt finding

The formation authenticated its source and slice bytes but then converted
heuristic discovery into successful semantic state. That boundary is
load-bearing. A provisional formation may remain incomplete; it may not label
known ambiguity, truncated context, or a false reference target as an exact,
non-RED join and then feed those rows into ownership and cost.

The two independent challenges found orthogonal manifestations of the same
failure:

1. context does not propagate through Bikeshed/legacy section structure, so
   informative explanations, history/change-log prose, and term-only `<dt>`
   slices become unflagged normative candidates;
2. ATX headings and several algorithm/serialization structures are missed, so
   exact source authentication is incorrectly adjacent to incomplete operation
   boundaries;
3. multi-domain functions and same-symbol/multi-owner rows are silently forced
   to one family rather than made RED;
4. scoped references are stripped and lossy slugs collapse distinct names;
   `[=set/Remove=]` is consequently joined to unrelated Web Animations
   `remove` definitions and labeled exact;
5. multiple target carrier occurrences cease to be ambiguous when their
   heuristic owner strings coincide, while target carrier IDs and original
   scoped spellings are omitted from the emitted edge;
6. identical source intervals can contribute several weighted carrier roles,
   so the cost lattice is a count of heuristic extraction events rather than
   reviewed operations;
7. two owner-scope rows lack exact ADDENDA input lines but are marked joined,
   and compatibility ownership is decided by symbol-name regex.

Root confirms an additional direct architecture conflict in the compatibility
join: `parseTimingFunction` and `CssTimingFunction` are assigned to
`keyframes` instead of the acknowledged `easing` family, while `ParseIssue`
and `ParseResult` are assigned to primitive-only `tokens`. Those rows are
provisional in prose but are not RED in the machine payload. They cannot enter
an owner graph.
