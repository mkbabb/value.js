OPERATIONALIZATION (2026-07-22).** This addendum records the owner's direct
rejection of the atom/token-object/CST architecture and the owner's replacement

orthogonally begotten prototypes for every feature, five independent hostile

## 1. Architectural law

### 1.1 Direct combinators over source productions

Every grammar is built directly from published `@mkbabb/parse-that@1.0.0`
primitives and `Parser` composition. `string`, `regex`, `whitespace`, `any`,
`all`, `dispatch`, `lazy`, `chain`, `map`, `mapState`, `skip`, `next`, `opt`,
`many`, `sepBy`, `eof`, `recover`, and parse-local state are available tools.
A regex used as a terminal parser is not by itself a lexer. A separately
materialized token stream, atom algebra, token-object layer, generic CST, or
feature-local/manual source scanner is forbidden.

CSS Syntax behavior still matters: preprocessing, escapes, comments, numeric
boundaries, blocks, functions, recovery, spans, and source preservation must
be expressed as composable grammar productions or parser-boundary results.
They may not be evaded merely because no token objects exist.

### 1.2 BBNF structural isomorphism

The final TypeScript grammar tree follows the acknowledged BBNF production
families and import direction where those boundaries are sound:

`tokens`, `value-unit`, `color`, `func-body`, `gradients`, `easing`, `filters`,
`keyframes`, `keywords`, `media`, `properties`, `selectors`, `transforms`,
`values`, and `stylesheet`.

These names are production-family **namespaces**, not a one-file ceiling.
Ledger-derived operation modules may nest beneath them to prevent god files.
This is production-family isomorphism, not blind file copying. The current
BBNF stylesheet root reaches only nine of those fifteen modules; `values`,
`gradients`, `transforms`, `filters`, `easing`, and `keyframes` are unreachable.
The final TypeScript roots must make every claimed family reachable or declare
and justify a separate public/internal entry root. Duplicated BBNF math,
function, and keyframes ownership is a defect to resolve, not a pattern to
transpose. The corrected DAG is itself content-addressed and acknowledged by
both tasks before it becomes a shared coordination boundary. `tokens` remains
a grammar-production namespace only, never a token-object runtime.

### 1.3 Parsimony and semantic boundaries

- Grammar modules own recognition and grammar-shaped values.
- Conversion, evaluation, cascade, DOM matching, and computed-value work stay
  in explicit semantic modules and never masquerade as grammar.
- Candidate-local helpers stay local. After two accepted feature cells
  independently exhibit the same production, helper promotion becomes its own
  feature row and must pass H/B/S, quintetto, synthesis triumvirate, and an
  integrated A/B proof before consumers migrate. Duplication before then is
  evidence, not a defect.
- No `new Function`, raw whole-remainder adapter, permissive `[^)]*` escape
  hatch, or balanced-text scanner may close a feature.
- Public compatibility adapters may wrap the winning grammar after grammar
  adjudication; they may not distort the grammar to preserve a live bug.

## 2. Feature denominator

The three-prototype rule applies to every row of a materialized **feature
ledger**, not merely to broad waves and not to every named constant. A feature
row is the smallest independently specifiable grammar operation with one input
language, one grammar-shaped result, one diagnostic/recovery contract, one
serialization contract when applicable, and one benchmark door.

Each row must contain:

1. stable feature ID and owning BBNF production family;
2. pinned normative production/algorithm references;
3. positive, negative, boundary, hostile, and round-trip fixture identities;
4. public/internal entry roots and output shape;
5. diagnostic, recovery, source-preservation, and work-limit behavior;
6. LIVE, spec, browser, and acknowledged-BBNF oracle roles;
7. three candidate paths and hashes;
8. one correctness-qualified benchmark cell and comparator identities;
9. five skeptic verdicts, three adjudicator verdicts, and final disposition.

The ledger begins with the acknowledged fifteen-family BBNF assay, the frozen
52-export/37-consumer compatibility surface, and the pinned July-2026
normative/experimental corpus. It is complete only when every normative
operation is mapped exactly once or explicitly marked out-of-language with a
reason. The BBNF tree's 276 declarations are evidence for decomposition, not a
substitute for this normative closure.

Before feature fan-out, a mechanical freeze records the exact specification
manifest (URL/path, revision, retrieval date, and content hash), owner-
experimental IDs, normative production/algorithm occurrence matrix,
52-export/37-consumer mapping, and a bijection from every occurrence to one
feature row or explicit exclusion. Two independent denominator challenges and
one gestalt adjudication must accept the exact ledger hash. A later row
split/merge/add/remove is an E-3 change; candidate authors never define their
own feature boundary. The pilot may prepare candidates only after its own row,
source occurrences, and fixtures receive the same boundary challenge; no pilot
acceptance or broad fan-out precedes full-ledger freeze.

## 3. Three orthogonal prototypes per feature

Every feature row receives at least these three independently authored
prototypes. They share only pinned specifications, fixture bytes, a minimal
ledger-frozen **observable result contract**, and the published parse-that
package. Candidate-internal shapes are unconstrained; candidate-local adapters
to the observation contract are included in reviewed and benchmarked bytes.
They do **not** share parser
helpers, grammar source, benchmark results, or one another's design notes
before author close.

| candidate | independent provenance | required character |
|---|---|---|
| **H — historical transpose** | the earlier direct-combinator `value.js` / `keyframes.js` implementation | preserve its readable parser shape where sound; remove scanners, unsafe evaluation, permissive fallbacks, monoliths, and incomplete CSS |
| **B — BBNF-production transpose** | the acknowledged `.bbnf` module and import family | express productions nearly one-for-one with parse-that combinators; repair unreachable/duplicated ownership rather than copying it |
| **S — specification-first** | pinned CSS specifications and browser/spec witnesses, without consulting H or B implementation source | choose the clearest direct parse-that grammar from the normative algorithm; expose common-mode errors in both historical and BBNF candidates |

Orthogonality is about provenance and construction, not cosmetic syntax. Three
files that call the same shared recognizer are one prototype, not three.
Candidate authors must record parse-that APIs used, LOC, backtracking shape,
allocations of material intermediate structures, and known compromises.

Before authoring, record a provenance graph and pairwise construction review.
If H and B share ancestry—or independence cannot be demonstrated—one is
comparator evidence rather than an orthogonal seat and a fourth engine-native
clean-room candidate is mandatory. Candidate topology must also differ
materially (for example production-tree `all`/`any`/`sepBy`, prefix-directed
`chain`/`dispatch`, and independently derived spec branches); labels alone do
not create orthogonality.

All three run the same born-RED fixture corpus and the same correctness-gated
microbenchmark. Failure is evidence, not permission to quietly converge the
candidates before skeptical review.

Three distinct, context-isolated author seats are mandatory. No author may
review or adjudicate that feature; no person/agent occupies two skeptic or two
adjudicator seats. Every author receipt records served model, input-manifest
hash, isolated candidate-root hash, commands, and close time. A candidate may
import only the exact package, sealed observation contract, and fixture
harness—never another candidate or candidate-local helper.

A mechanical anti-scanner gate rejects retired-tree imports and typed grammar
source containing manual cursor/source slicing, `state.src`, `indexOf`,
`balancedUntil`, `splitTopLevel`, generic token/component/CST aggregates, or
broad remainder fallbacks such as `[^)]*` and `[^;{}]*`. A narrowly scoped
normative terminal algorithm requires the explicit waiver in `MODULE-DAG.md`;
it can return one semantic primitive, never a token stream.

## 4. Quintetto: five hostile skeptic passes

After all three candidate authors close, five independent skeptics receive the
same hashed candidate set and oracle corpus. Each begins with the presumption
that all three are incorrect, unidiomatic, and slow. They do not see one
another's work before verdict seal.

Every skeptic covers the same three-altitude, all-axis rubric: total-tranche
denominator and sequencing fit; feature-cell boundary and deliverables; then,
for **each** candidate, correctness, parse-that idiom, performance, hostile
behavior, and KISS/LOC. Every skeptic returns ACCEPT/REJECT per candidate on
all common axes before ranking. The named roles below are depth specialties,
not permission to omit another axis.

1. **Specification skeptic** — normative grammar, algorithms, browser witness,
   differential classifications, serialization, and missing feature scope.
2. **Parse-that skeptic** — direct combinator idiom, API use, backtracking,
   needless regex/manual parsing, abstraction quality, and BBNF boundary fit.
3. **Hostility skeptic** — no-throw behavior, malformed and adversarial input,
   diagnostics, recovery, source spans, limits, recursion, and fuzz corpus.
4. **Performance skeptic** — comparable corpora, semantic prechecks, raw
   measurements, warmup/order bias, allocations, complexity, and benchmark
   gaming.
5. **Gestalt skeptic** — public compatibility, consumer seams, module graph,
   cross-feature duplication, KISS/LOC, and whether the feature boundary itself
   is wrong.

Each verdict ranks all candidates, names blocking defects per candidate, and
may return `REJECT_ALL`. A confirmed semantic defect or non-idiomatic/manual
scanner rejects that candidate. Two accepting opinions cannot outvote a
confirmed defect.

Blocking claims enter a reproducible docket. A semantic blocker needs an exact
counterexample and normative/oracle classification; a scanner/idiom blocker
needs exact source lines and the violated architectural rule; a performance
blocker needs raw paired evidence under §6. Reproduction against the sealed
hash confirms it. Disputed normative classification goes to the semantic
adjudicator and pinned specification, never a vote. No open confirmed blocker
reaches selection. Reviewers add executable counterexamples whenever the
claim is behavioral.

Before authoring, a skeptic custodian seals the hash of a hostile/fuzz holdout
made without candidate-source access while withholding its bytes from authors.
After candidate close the bytes are revealed and run against all three. The
precommitted hash and results belong to that generation; a mismatch or failure
blocks acceptance.

This quintetto **subsumes E-1's two-audit minimum** and its common rubric
contains E-1's three altitudes. It is not followed by ceremonial duplicate
audits. The **formation triumvirate/E-3** governs normative pins, ledger
boundaries, observable result contracts, module ownership, and comparator
identities. Ordinary code repair within a frozen row uses a generation restart,
not an addendum. The **synthesis triumvirate** in §5 selects code only.

Any candidate byte, observation contract, fixture, holdout, comparator, or
corpus change creates generation N+1, invalidates triplet rankings, and reruns
all five reviews. `REJECT_ALL` or a repaired candidate likewise starts a new
triplet. A candidate may be withdrawn, but a winner still needs five unblocked
verdicts. Receipt-only formatting outside hashed inputs does not restart.

## 5. Triumvirate and final apotheosis

Only a quintetto-complete feature set reaches three independent adjudicators:

1. **semantic adjudicator** — selects the spec-correct grammar/result contract;
2. **parser-architecture adjudicator** — selects the most idiomatic, readable,
   modular parse-that construction;
3. **performance/gestalt adjudicator** — selects the simplest candidate that
   survives comparable performance and integration evidence.

Each independently nominates one already quintetto-reviewed exact candidate
tree hash before seeing the other two. If all three nominate the same hash,
they attest it and the root only records that result. The root cannot override
dissent or mint unseen bytes. Any composite, edit, wrapper, or aggregate tree
whose hash was not reviewed is a new candidate generation and must rerun the
common corpus, benchmark, all five skeptic passes, and all three adjudications.

The final feature is `APOTHEOSIS-ACCEPTED` only when:

- all blocking skeptic defects are discharged;
- all three adjudicators accept the exact final hash;
- the full feature corpus, no-throw/limit rail, round-trip rail, and public
  adapter differential are green;
- for every genuinely comparable door, the final candidate satisfies the
  predeclared strict-win rule against the LIVE regex and every retained prior
  iteration, with no correctness regression;
- its module/import placement matches the adjudicated BBNF family lattice.

Integration cannot make an unaccepted feature accepted. After integration,
cross-feature and full-surface differentials, hostile tests, and benchmarks run
again; a new interaction defect reopens every owning feature row.

## 6. Benchmark law, kept deliberately small

Every candidate is measured only after semantic prechecks pass. Per-feature
and full-surface runs use identical immutable input bytes and operations for:

- H, B, and S candidates;
- the winning apotheosis grammar;
- the identified live regex variant;
- retained prior parse-that iterations; and
- any acknowledged, locally verified parse-that engine uplift.

Before first timing, seal a content-addressed run manifest and immutable raw
result path. Record runtime/engine, hardware, source/corpus/operation hashes,
warmup, sample count, an interleaved paired schedule, raw timings, invocations,
environment, exits, failures, and throughput unit. No retry may erase a valid
failure; pairwise denominators and schedule identities are immutable. Every
operation validates normalized results before timing. A predeclared one-sided
paired confidence interval for candidate/peer time must lie wholly below 1.0
for a strict win. Retain append-only raw JSON and report median plus dispersion.
Allocation/peak-memory evidence is required for allocation-sensitive rows or
marked `UNAVAILABLE` with the tool reason. No UTF-16 code-unit result may be
relabeled UTF-8 MB/s.

The target remains a strict correctness-preserving win over both the regex
variant and the previous iterations. A peer without semantics-equivalent
operations is `NON_COMPARABLE`, supports no broad win claim, and cannot close
G-3. Measurement may expose a loss; it may not relax CSS semantics. The huge
unimplemented `ADDENDA-06` lifecycle is retired, while its sound minimum—unit
identity, pairwise denominator, schedule identity, immutable raw evidence, and
no retry erasure—is retained here.

Each feature uses one machine-verifiable bundle rather than another prose
forest:

```text

cells/<feature-id>/fixtures/manifest.json
cells/<feature-id>/candidates/{h,b,s}/

```

One validator checks hashes, seats, generations, evidence, verdict completeness,
and exact-hash acceptance, then generates tranche summaries. Duplicate wave or
addendum prose is forbidden unless an E-3 formation change actually occurs.
