### 2.1 Conflict rulings

1. **Compatibility versus clean break.** The frozen 52-export/37-consumer
   surface remains an observation and eventual adapter target. It does not
   authorize legacy parser internals, dual paths, aliases, or grammar
   distortion.
2. **Three-by-five-by-three versus direct work.** The owner law remains
   binding, but its unit is the coherent feature operation defined in
   `ADDENDA-07 §2`, not every terminal constant or evidence field. Audit
   machinery must be shared and small; it may not become the product. Any
   proposed regrouping, exemption, or weaker granularity is a new
   owner/addendum decision, not a handoff interpretation.
3. **Full denominator versus early implementation.** Denominator work and
   grammar work proceed together. A feature needs a pinned local boundary
   before candidates, but the whole CSS ledger is not a precondition for
   building every independently sound foundation family.
4. **Live parser versus specification.** The live parser is the primary
   differential comparator on its actual domain; the pinned specification
   arbitrates divergence, and browser CSSOM is a third witness.
5. **Historical model names.** Fable/Opus route labels in older documents are
   historical requirements, not truthful receipts for Codex-served models.
   Every new seat records the actual served model and reasoning configuration.
6. **Browser tooling.** The owner’s earlier specific preference for the
   internal Browser supersedes the old handoff’s unconditional Playwright
   wording. Use standalone Playwright only when the internal Browser cannot
   perform or preserve the exact required witness, and record why.
