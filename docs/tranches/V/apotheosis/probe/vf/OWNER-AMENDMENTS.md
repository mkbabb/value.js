## Deletion judgment

Every deletion decision answers four falsifiable questions:

1. What intrinsic job, independent of consumer count, does the surface perform?
2. Which retained primitive or application-owned composition performs every worthy
   job with less conceptual and runtime cost?
3. Which exact imports, runtime behaviors, data formats and documentation claims
   are casualties, and which named wave migrates each before terminal deletion?
4. Does the packed capability diff contain a by-name tombstone and zero alias,
   shim, fallback, source link or dual path?

Failure of questions 2–4 blocks deletion. Failure to prove a unique intrinsic job
favors deletion; age, LOC and raw consumer count do not decide either direction.
