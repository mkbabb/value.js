# Formation Audit Registry

## Control law

This ledger accounts for the owner-directed 32-agent formation audit. Every
seat requested `gpt-5.6-sol` at `ultra`, and its persisted session content
attests that route; early full-history spawns inherited the request from an
evidenced Sol/ultra parent turn, while later no-history spawns declared it
explicitly. Provider-authenticated session existence, freshness, independence,
model, and effort are an external trust boundary and are not claimed here. A hostile pair begins from the premise that
the assigned formation slice is wrong; a third fresh Sol adjudicates mechanisms
against file/line evidence and returns exact formation amendments. Completion
reports without a named defect, reproduction, falsifier, or proof are rejected.

The desktop runtime permits three child agents beside root, so the 32-seat
budget is rotated through bounded cells of at most three children. The requested
5–6 concurrency is therefore capability-limited and is never claimed as actual
simultaneous service; two persisted thread-limit failures prove the live limit.
Concurrency limits change scheduling, not the thrice contract. Production
source remains held throughout.

[`FORMATION-SEAT-LEDGER.json`](FORMATION-SEAT-LEDGER.json) is the binding machine
record. It joins each numbered seat to the exact successful spawn call, parent
turn, child JSONL, child thread ID, content-attested requested model/effort,
final report, immutable session hash, batch, panel, role, iteration, finding
family, and outcome.
[`tools/validate-seat-ledger.mjs`](tools/validate-seat-ledger.mjs) rejects
duplicate seats/sessions, route drift, incomplete closed triads, iteration four,
missing `formation-root` escalation after iteration three, and false capacity
claims. The table below is its human projection.

## Stability and clean-pass law

After each content-attested third-Sol decision, accepted mechanisms are folded
into the owning wave and rejected mechanisms retain the proof of rejection. A new family or a
material amendment resets the consecutive-clean counter. Formation closes only
after every seat above has a terminal disposition and two later, rotated,
whole-corpus hostile triads independently report no new family, orphan demand,
vacuous gate, or unsupported global claim. Those are formation receipts only.
C08 and C09 repeat the law after production execution; they neither consume
nor inherit a scoped audit seat or formation-pass green.

No cluster may silently enter iteration four. After skeptic A, skeptic B, and
the content-attested third-Sol decision consume iterations one through three, any surviving or
new mechanism escalates to the named `formation-root` owner, receives a new
finding-family disposition, and resets the clean-pass counter.

The first attempted whole-formation pass surfaced `canonical-exec-liveness`
before it could close; the later Agent-v2 assay sustained prompt/custody,
challenge-order, mandatory-consumer and admin-Breath defects as RR-15 through
RR-18. Those attempts are evidence, not clean receipts. They contribute zero
of the two required passes, leave production execution at **0/190**, and
require one fresh focused hostile pair/adjudication before a new epoch freezes.
