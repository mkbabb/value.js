## Decisions caused by the rubric

- Preserve the strong extant value composition; refine proportion, semantics,
  route state, responsive geometry and micro-motion rather than reskin it.
- Freeze each app's source-derived material palette, three-voice type register,
  responsive composition grammar and one domain-specific signature gesture so
  a later wave cannot replace observed identity with a generic cream/serif/glass
  template while claiming visual improvement.
- Treat keyframes mobile as a complete single-screen stage, not a compressed
  desktop sidebar.
- Replace the Easing pill wall with a ruled chronograph contact sheet whose
  information density, curve fidelity and event geometry are independently
  measurable.
- Separate paint bounds from event bounds: watercolor and curve ink may remain
  delicate while semantic seats remain reachable.
- Admit Breath only through named effects, bounded amplitude, one clock owner,
  visibility parking and PRM removal.
- Preserve the existing AuroraAtoms job and the complete BlobConfig surface;
  progressive disclosure follows total field disposition rather than visual
  simplification by deletion.
- Abrogate shadcn-era consumer structure completely; missing shared jobs are
  designed in Glass idioms and then consumed through public Glass contracts.
