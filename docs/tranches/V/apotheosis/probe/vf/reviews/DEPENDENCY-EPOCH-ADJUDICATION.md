## Sustained law

The root return alone proves the current checkout and may complete. Every
unique dependency is an immutable historical certificate at its own clean
committed epoch. Structural offline inspection proves neither acceptance nor
decision authority. A decision becomes authoritative only when a specialized
typed composer joins the immutable owner certificate, allowed edge status,
total decision rows and one composition-local identity per wave.

The four proof predicates are deliberately disjoint:

| Validation class | accepted at epoch | holds now | authorizes decision | may complete |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Structural offline | no | no | no | no |
| Immutable historical certificate | yes | no | no | no |
| Typed decision composition | yes | no | yes | no |
| Default live root | yes | yes | no | yes |

Historical replay materializes the recorded clean commit, preserves the
logical repository identity, verifies the complete pin/state certificate,
proof-manifest bytes, executable and fully emitted environment, executes the
canonical gate, compares exit/stdout/stderr and proves no post-gate mutation.
Dirty historical authority is rejected rather than acquiring a second snapshot
protocol.

## Sustained findings

1. Live-recursing a predecessor after its dependent legitimately advances the
   same repository counterfeits the predecessor epoch.
2. Relabelling `--offline` as historical replay while skipping pins,
   executable/environment, proof-manifest and command execution is a
   close-class lie.
3. A self-hashed receipt establishes integrity only; it does not prove a gate
   ran or authorize a decision.
4. One-wave/one-identity is composition-local. `A1 -> B1` remains truthful
   history after `A2`; a new composition may not consume both A1 and A2.
5. A hostile triad closes `wave@epoch`. Unrelated downstream mutation does not
   erase it; mutation of its owned state/evidence or a changed relied-on
   invariant reopens it.
6. Static DAG status is edge-specific. `REFUSED` is non-advancing and belongs
   only to typed reopening supersession.
7. Challenge prompts and actor-authored reports must bind wave, contract,
   complete dependency closure, implementation-state vector, gate contract,
   all three gestalt axes/evidence and, for the adjudicator, both critic report
   paths, hashes and contents.
8. Validator-control environment variables must never reach a proof command;
   the environment digest covers every emitted variable.
9. V29T, K22T and M10T need additional typed before/after proof, but none is an
   exception to the universal historical law.
10. Keyframes decision owners must be historical authorities whose complete
    closure identities join the root/direct-dependency closure; plain offline
    owner inspection permits split ancestry.
