## Event-region and easing decisions

G08 is internal substrate, not a public polymorphic `InteractionSeat`. Paint
and event geometry are independent. Coarse seats have a ≥44×44 px floor;
fine-pointer floors are component-specific. Edge, corner, perimeter, z-order,
disabled, clipped, zoom/DPR and `elementFromPoint` probes must prove the event
region reaches only its semantic owner and never overlaps or occludes a
neighbor.

G09 is the sole shared easing authoring and glyph producer:

- `CurveGlyph` consumes the typed V25 easing and the declared rendered box.
- Cubic easing is one exact SVG cubic; `steps()` uses exact horizontal and
  vertical segments with its jump term; `linear()` uses exact piecewise stops.
- Other callable easings use iterative adaptive subdivision with geometric
  deviation ≤0.5 physical pixel at actual dimensions and DPR 1, 2 and 3.
- Node/work limits are frozen and limit/+1 returns typed `resource_limit`;
  fixed 32/48-sample fallback is forbidden.
- The complete catalogue shares one frozen y-domain, independent of filters.
  Certified extrema over `[0,1]` produce
  `raw=[min(0,minima),max(1,maxima)]`; padding is
  `max(0.05,0.05*span)`, then both bounds round outward to `0.05`. Cubics use
  derivative roots, steps use discontinuity limits, and linear uses exact
  breakpoints. Adaptive callables supply an interval/error certificate rather
  than samples alone. Nonfinite/uncertifiable extrema or bounds outside
  `[-8,8]` return typed `glyph_domain_exceeded`; clamping and filter rescaling
  are forbidden. Strokes remain vector and non-scaling; raster previews are
  forbidden.
- The Picker is truly controlled and updates from parent writes without keyed
  remount. Exact cubic fields, step count/jump, pointer, keyboard, reset, zoom
  and copy remain coherent. Layout is container-driven; consumer deep
  selectors and `!important` overrides are forbidden.
