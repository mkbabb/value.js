# Live Visual Formation Audit

## Status and method

This is formation evidence captured on 2026-07-18 from the deployed applications,
not a substitute for the pinned `π` and `DELTA` corpus required by D25, M11 and
C07.  Each route was loaded directly, allowed to settle, and inspected through
the rendered geometry and accessibility tree. The handset pass used 390 × 844;
the desktop pass used 1440 × 900. The tables count a painted/control box below
44 CSS px as a compact-paint observation, not automatically as a failed event
region: G08 deliberately permits compact paint inside a separate ≥44 px
semantic seat. A target failure is asserted only when hit-testing or semantic
geometry proves the effective seat small, collapsed, clipped or unreachable.
Decorative and hidden-editor geometry is reported separately from document
overflow.

The deployed applications audited were:

- `https://color.babb.dev/` — all 14 canonical value routes;
- `https://keyframes.babb.dev/` — Home plus all six canonical scenes.

These measurements are born-RED composition and reachability witnesses; the
paint counts alone are not target-size verdicts. They do not authorize
execution or claim closure across the full compact/tablet/zoom/theme/PRM/
forced-color/device matrix.

### Lost tools bar witness

On Picker/Home the unique `Toggle action bar` button paints at about 48 × 40.
Activating it left `aria-pressed=false`; the associated toolbar containers
remained width `0`, opacity `0`, and exposed no semantic action items.  The
button is therefore an empty affordance on that route, not merely a small
toolbar. D03A owns restoration through one route-independent capability
registration contract.

### Gradient strip witness

The 1,475 px specimen row is inside an intended horizontal scroll owner, so it
does not enlarge the document.  Its current continuation affordance and tiny
20 px stops nevertheless fail touch reachability and selector legibility.  D16
must prove the internal scroll law, reachability and stop edit geometry rather
than claiming `document.scrollWidth === clientWidth` as closure.

### Easing redesign decree

The mobile Easing selector is not a token-polish job.  The current capsules are
too rounded and large, while the curve marks are too coarse and low-density for
comparison.  M08 must replace the selector architecture with:

- a bounded family chooser instead of a horizontally clipped rail;
- a contact sheet with two columns at 320 px and three at 390/430 px, sharp
  ruled cells whose corner radius is no greater than 4 CSS px, and at least
  six/nine simultaneously complete choices respectively;
- exact vector cubic-Bézier/steps/`linear()` glyphs and adaptive paths for other
  admitted curves with transformed deviation at most 0.5 physical pixel at
  the actual box on one comparison domain at DPR 1/2/3;
- readable labels and semantic event regions of at least 44 × 44 CSS px whose
  paint remains compact rather than inflating into capsules;
- exact selected, focus, copy, keyboard and two-pointer viewport states.

Catalogue glyphs remain static; only the selected curve may perform one
user-triggered sweep. Fixed 32/48-sample duplicate producers, per-card frame
loops and decorative catalogue motion fail the wave. A DELTA that merely
shrinks, flattens or retokens the existing pill grid also fails.

### Cube gesture witness

The current Cube binds Pointer Events, Touch Events and Safari Gesture Events in
parallel at `demo/scenes/cube/orbital-drag/OrbitalDrag.vue:279`.  The touch lane
derives distance, centroid and twist independently in
`composables/useOrbitalPinch.ts:55`, while the pointer lane maintains a second
active-pointer and 1→2→1 state machine in `composables/useOrbitalPointer.ts:50`.
This is a concrete double-authority risk, especially on Safari, and cannot prove
the requested custom quaternion multi-touch contract.  K12/M05 must converge on
one PointerEvent-owned kernel with exact capture, cancel, lost-capture, rebase,
centroid, pinch and twist laws; C07 supplies real iOS and Android transcripts.
