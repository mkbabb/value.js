## Novel mechanisms excluded from V-next

The separate session, not V-next, may investigate and adjudicate:

- opaque functional parser versus class/run-context representations;
- a new public discriminated result model or raw-execution removal;
- new `cut`, `attempt`, `label`, recovery, span, and custom-leaf families;
- per-run memo table redesign and left-recursion strategy;
- core/diagnostic/memo/debug entrypoint and package-condition redesign;
- ESM-only publication or a new cross-format interoperability policy;
- generic Tape/SoA/byte substrates;
- Pratt facilities for bounded precedence grammars;
- BBNF module IR, namespaces, recursive groups, host capability purity,
  emitter ABI, stable symbol ids, recovery ABI, and source maps;
- Rust/VM/TypeScript/WASM backend equivalence and generated-artifact policy;
- independent-oracle versus shared-runtime-witness architecture;
- a major parse-that/BBNF release or coordinated version reset.

No item above may be smuggled into a V-next blocker fix. If a CSS prototype
appears to require one, the P-band must either find a smaller repair or refuse
and route the exact blocker here.
