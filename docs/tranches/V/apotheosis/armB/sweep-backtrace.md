## 3. THE GLASS-UI BREATH OF LIFE — transferable motion/affordance principles (named)

Sources: BJ `FEEDBACK-LEDGER.md` (F01-F50, A01-A17); `IOS27-MICRO/CHARTER.md` (the exhortation §1-§7);
`IOS27-MICRO/analysis/NOVELTY-ROSTER.md` (the post-critique canon; verified-model claude-fable-5).
The 13 principles most transferable to the value.js demo, each with its glass anchor:

1. **BREATH-OF-LIFE (the law over every row).** Every component ALWAYS displays engagement; when
   engaged it expresses that state meaningfully, with proper state relayed to the user, for every
   variant (NOVELTY-ROSTER:19-20; FEEDBACK A01/A11). This is the umbrella edict.
2. **THE GOAL OF GLASS.** Glass reads BLURRED and FROSTED — never trite, shiny, or bright; tint does
   the depth work, brightness never amplifies, specular is engagement-only (NOVELTY-ROSTER:20-26,
   §3.5-A canon register). Kills the "shiny glass" failure the owner flagged (FEEDBACK F19/F48).
3. **CHOREOGRAPHED, NEVER NAIVELY BATCHED — "fade faster than stretch."** Multi-dimensional animation
   at different, dynamic points; the fade happens faster than the transform; this asymmetry IS the
   breath of life (CHARTER §5). The single highest-value transfer for value's panels/toasts.
4. **MARK THE MOMENTUM — velocity/acceleration tracking.** Every component tracks momentum, velocity,
   acceleration of its curves; springiness; overshoot is VELOCITY-BOUGHT, not decorative
   (CHARTER §6; NOVELTY M4 "overshoot is velocity-BOUGHT").
5. **MAGNETIC OVERPULL / bounded elasticity.** A surface may be pulled past its maximal position but
   is magnetically bounded like a bézier control point — alive but real (CHARTER §2). Transfers to
   value sliders/drawers/easing-curve editor.
6. **DOCK-TO-CARD GROWTH with edge squish-stretch.** A surface grows into a card, squishing/stretching
   at bottom + left/right edges, growing slightly horizontally as it expands vertically (CHARTER §1;
   NOVELTY V-BLACKDOCK/V-MORPHDOCK). Transfers to any value panel that expands from a control.
7. **TWO-REGISTERS-TWO-INTENTS.** Gesture-scrubbed motion rides a critically-damped register (ζ≈0.82,
   overshoot velocity-bought); FIRED/programmatic deploys ride an underdamped panel spring (ζ0.71,
   f_d≈1.75 Hz, intrinsic 4-5% overshoot) (NOVELTY §3.5, card 7 V-BLACKDOCK, X-CHOREO `panel` preset).
8. **THE ENGAGE-ENVELOPE REGISTER — one attack, per-role release.** First-order opacity/luminance
   envelopes own the LIGHT/MEDIUM channels; springs own GEOMETRY; an envelope never moves a rect, a
   spring never dims a lens. Press-acknowledge attack 40-60 ms (t90 ≤150 ms) (NOVELTY §3.5-B).
9. **THE LULL FILAMENT — never fall to zero.** A live surface falls to a becalmed line, never to
   zero; realized as a CSS-only compositor breath over a parked canvas — zero idle rAF
   (NOVELTY V-WAVE / V-DOTREL, law-11; §3.5). Directly answers value's "watercolor dots always
   alive" (C10).
10. **THE COMMIT HARD-CUT.** flare to maximum → pin ~120-170 ms → HARD CUT into the next surface — the
    one licensed sharpness, meaning "heard you" (NOVELTY V-WAVE card 5).
11. **ANIMATE LAYER OPACITY UNDER FIXED BLUR RADII — never the radius.** Progressive/gradient blur =
    3-4 stacked backdrop-filter layers under static gradient masks, σ ≈ doubling per band; cap the
    stack at 3, no clipping wrapper (NOVELTY §1 survey; §3.5-E `--glass-halo-*`). The load-bearing
    PERFORMANCE law for any glass blur in value.
12. **ZERO IDLE COST / SAFARI-HONEST GATES.** No per-frame JS layout reads, animated blur radii,
    runtime SVG turbulence, or unbounded backdrop surfaces; rAF dies at settle; `@supports(backdrop-
    filter:url())` LIES → runtime paint-probe gates + video as the honest WebKit organ, no
    Chrome-special behavior (NOVELTY §1, honesty fence). Transfers to value's π/probe discipline.
13. **THE MEDIUM-RECRUITMENT DOSE COLUMN — who may soften the world.** sliders/steppers = full halo;
    text fields under focus = whisper; nav/lens chrome = the lens IS medium; buttons/toggles/data
    reads/substrates = NONE by law (NOVELTY §3.5-D). A ready-made rule for value's control affordances.
