## Execution prohibition

No production implementation begins until:

1. all formation artifacts are persisted;
2. repository pins and dirty state are refreshed;
3. every wave has mission, dependencies, born-RED witness, deliverables, falsifiable gates and exclusions;
4. every prompt demand maps to an owning wave;
5. two consecutive fresh hostile formation passes remain clean.

Earlier formation passes were invalidated by the renewed hostile review. Two
fresh formation-wide triads must run after the 32-seat audit and final accepted
amendment. They are recorded as formation evidence only. C08 and C09 are
distinct future execution-closure waves after all implementation owners close;
no formation review is credited to them. Formation work may create its
expressly specified prototype only after the relevant hostile pair and
third-Sol adjudication converge; production source remains held.

## Per-wave method

Every implementation wave follows:

1. re-pin and read only its packet;
2. reproduce the born-RED witness;
3. implement the smallest coherent architectural cut;
4. run targeted tests and proportionate broader gates;
5. inspect graph, public surface and consumer effects;
6. collect π/DELTA for visual claims;
7. freeze the exact final repository-state projection and run two fresh
   single-turn hostile Sol-ultra critics, each covering tranche fit and
   optimality, wave-contract adherence and friction, and feature behavior;
8. after both critics finish, run one fresh Sol-ultra adjudicator over their
   content-addressed reports to agglomerate, prove or disprove, and ameliorate;
9. repair and route every surviving finding, then discard the stale triad and
   repeat steps 7–8 whenever the pinned state changed;
10. return the universal contract.

The terminal return binds only the final clean pair and ratified adjudication
against the live state. This is the required two-pass challenge plus the third
adjudicator—not two duplicate triads. Batch and closure audits may challenge
earlier work again, but never substitute for this local close.
