## Shared π contract

Every implementation wave captures its own born-RED visual and interaction evidence, then proves its affected surface across:

- desktop `1440×900`;
- tablet `768×1024`;
- mobile `390×844`;
- compact mobile `320×568`;
- supported light/dark themes;
- `200%` and `400%` zoom;
- mouse, touch, keyboard, and assistive semantics;
- default and `prefers-reduced-motion`;
- current supported Chromium, Firefox, and Safari engines.

A feature wave cannot defer its first responsive, accessibility, animation-energy, or route-state proof to D25. D25 audits completed work and reopens the owning wave; it never repairs.

The value demo remains the **Optical Instrumentarium**. Refinement preserves its identity while adding restrained material response, precise spatial rhythm, deterministic procedural life, and truthful interaction.
