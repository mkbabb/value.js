## Conflict protocol

1. A peer head moving is expected. Re-read its published or frozen contract
   immediately before adoption.
2. If two active worktrees claim the same source file, package export, grammar
   production, registry identity, or dependency pin, stop the younger change
   and send one exact conflict packet naming both commits and paths.
3. A consumer defect stays with Value unless a packed producer contract fails.
   A source-only producer observation is not a release blocker.
4. No peer message repeats an acknowledged batch. Amend only with a new
   producer-root defect, changed contract, or exact ownership conflict.
5. BBNF receives one boundary receipt after W3 freezes. Until then, silence is
   the correct coordination state.
