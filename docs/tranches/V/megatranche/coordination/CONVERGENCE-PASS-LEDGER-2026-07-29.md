## Cross-pass rules

1. P2 opens from P1's terminal family registry, not from the original favored
   narrative.
2. A Luna packet has one bounded mechanical question and returns evidence to
   its repository owner through root.
3. A frontend pass is incomplete without internal-browser evidence or an exact
   environment limitation plus the source/browser gate that a later wave must
   close.
4. Package migrations prove a released tuple, built typings/exports, a
   deliberately fail-capable type gate, and consumer runtime behavior.
5. Clean A and clean B use fresh prompts and may not inherit a “done” summary.
6. A model-routing mismatch is not waived merely because the mechanical
   output looks plausible. The mismatched artifact remains evidence, and the
   bounded required-model replay must independently confirm or amend it.
