## 7. Canon: Golden Glass, Breath of Life, Movement of Momentum

### 7.1 Golden Glass

Golden Glass is **warm-neutral, luminous structural glass**. “Golden” names proportion, warmth, and
restraint. It does not require yellow tint, metallic shimmer, cartoon shadow, or compulsory phi
arithmetic.

1. Translucency is earned only where the backdrop materially informs the foreground.
2. Quiet reading and dense controls use the least material necessary, including opaque surfaces.
3. One scene uses one material stack and one light direction.
4. Borders carry edge separation; shadows carry elevation; neither duplicates the other.
5. Chromatic content is the protagonist. Glass frames it and never competes with it.

### 7.2 Breath of Life

Breath is a state contract, not perpetual motion:

| state | law |
|---|---|
| rest | the action and its identity are discoverable without hover |
| hover | reinforces the available action; never reveals the only affordance |
| focus | at least as prominent as hover, modality-visible, never clipped |
| press | grounds/compresses the control and preserves its target |
| selected | persists without hover and is not expressed by color alone |
| busy | retains identity, reports progress, and prevents repeat action |
| disabled | quiet but legible, with the reason available where consequential |

Ambient “breathing” parks when offscreen or invisible. Reduced motion removes it. The desktop dock
must therefore choose explicitly: an honest rest disclosure in the seal/persistent slot, or
always-expanded posture. Hover-only navigation is not an acceptable third state.

### 7.3 Movement of Momentum

Motion follows causality:

- **drivers**—dragged stops, expanding docks, thrown/inertial objects—may use spring or inertia;
- **observers**—route content, readouts, inspectors—use short crossfade/translate continuity;
- one effect has one clock owner;
- nested independent loops and universal `max-height` transitions are prohibited;
- route transitions preserve spatial origin and do not animate unrelated regions;
- reduced motion resolves directly to the settled hierarchy.

`demo/DESIGN.md` should be re-authored from these laws, not patched around its dead paths, literal
gold/cartoon voice, and stale shadcn instructions.
