## 5. Subject-specific design law

The audit uses the `frontend-design` skill as a formation rubric. Value helps
color practitioners author, compare, generate, transform, save, share, and
govern measured color. Its signature must therefore arise from measured chroma,
transformation, relationship, and provenance—not generic gradients, glass
cards, pill controls, or decorative motion.

Every route/page and every `VC-001`–`VC-088` workflow receives two distinct
design passes:

### Pass D1 — subject-specific brief

- name the user, page/component job, protagonist, decision, and success state;
- name the measured-color data or provenance that determines palette, tokens,
  contrast, layout, or motion;
- define display/body/utility typography, hierarchy, density, spacing,
  dividers, cards, controls, container behavior, and exact state copy;
- identify the one memorable signature and the one semantic motion owner;
- appraise **Golden Glass**, **Breath of Life**, and **Movement of Momentum**
  with concrete measured-chroma evidence or record a justified refusal;
- specify loading, empty, error, offline, auth/Admin, reduced-motion,
  forced-colors/platform-equivalent, RTL, keyboard, VoiceOver, and Dynamic Type.

### Pass D2 — independent critique

- test whether the result could be mistaken for a generic dashboard or stock
  component library;
- remove decoration that does not communicate color relationship, state,
  provenance, affordance, or hierarchy;
- challenge excessive cards, rounded containers, divider noise, duplicated
  labels, weak protagonist, generic copy, gratuitous gradients, and motion
  without state ownership;
- challenge paint layers that intercept input, hidden focus, unsafe targets,
  clipped popovers, keyboard occlusion, scroll traps, and reduced-motion drift;
- return terminal `KEEP/FOLD/MOVE/SPLIT/PRUNE` per concept and exact remediation
  receiver. It does not edit product source.

The two passes may share source evidence but not authorial judgment. A family
brief may establish vocabulary; it cannot replace the component-specific row.
