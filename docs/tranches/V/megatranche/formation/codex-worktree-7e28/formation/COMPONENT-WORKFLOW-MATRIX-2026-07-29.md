## 2. Per-component workflow contract

Every `VC-###` workflow performs, in order:

1. source/parent/child/style/state/DI/test/route/consumer graph census;
2. route job and protagonist statement;
3. D design challenge under Golden Glass, Breath of Life, and Movement of Momentum;
4. L library-grain challenge: KEEP/FOLD/MOVE/SPLIT/PRUNE, module-child filename de-duplication, export and consumer proof;
5. C implementation challenge: async, cleanup, error, loading, empty, offline, auth/Admin, ownership, and inaccessible-state proof;
6. isolated prototype or measurement if needed; prototypes never land as product source;
7. in-app Browser matrix: desktop, 390×844, short landscape, keyboard, reduced motion, forced colors, RTL, and sampled animation frames;
8. independent re-read, exact D/L/C hashes, validator bank, then wave admission.

Global visual gates inherited by all 88 rows:

- semantic heading and landmark hierarchy;
- cards only where grouping earns a surface;
- coherent spacing and deliberate dividers;
- controls name their effect and expose focus;
- interaction owners do not masquerade as paint owners;
- removable decoration has a measured purpose;
- measured-chroma signature belongs to the Value subject, not generic glass chrome;
- loading, empty, error, offline, auth, and Admin are distinct states rather than silent emptiness.

`ADJ` means the live ledger marks the row adjudicated. `BANK` means exact D/L/C are hash-banked. `OPEN(DLC)` and `OPEN(D)` are current live deficits.
