## 3. Visual thesis

Value’s subject is the relationship between measured color and authored color.
Its signature should come from chroma, interpolation, contrast, provenance, and
transformation—not from glass cards piled around generic controls.

### Golden Glass

- glass is a hierarchy material, not the subject;
- one dominant working plane earns the strongest material;
- secondary panes use quieter separation before another card;
- borders and dividers communicate grouping or state; removable hairlines die;
- controls remain optically legible under measured chroma and forced colors.

### Breath of Life

- direct manipulation changes the specimen immediately;
- authored values and visual output remain perceptually coupled;
- loading, empty, offline, error, auth, and Admin are distinct lived states;
- action feedback belongs to the action owner and never celebrates a no-op.

### Movement of Momentum

- animation follows the transformation’s semantic path;
- concurrent 200–550ms micro-transitions need one cadence;
- persistent pulses are reserved for changing or urgent state;
- reduced motion resolves to stable geometry and chroma, not 0.01ms “running”
  animations plus residual geometry transitions.
