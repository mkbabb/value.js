## What P3 actually proves

The direct scannerless closure surface is a useful terminal falsifier:

- warmed success projections measure only `1.0656x` to `1.5238x`;
- prewarmed construction measures `1.7118x` to `1.9534x`;
- the bounded failure-state subset measures `1.3459x` to `1.7557x`;
- grammar heap falls `40.5%`;
- retained parse state for the 96-statement fixture grows `12.4%`;
- unchanged-production `proof:perf` is RED at `+16.0%` against the `15%`
  guard (`2,022 ns` versus `1,742 ns`).

The result is fatal to the candidate even after correcting earlier overclaims.
It is **bounded correctness evidence**, not every-subject equivalence:

- complete parse state is not projected;
- failure-result projection is absent;
- invalid JSON is not proved exactly equal to the live M2 `jsonParser`;
- the JSON-shaped control uses M2 primitives but is not the live
  dispatch-based consumer;
- build-plus-first/cold and alternating-grammar-shape planes are absent;
- there is no exact bootstrap;
- scaled diagnostics-on recovered-success evidence is absent.

These omissions cannot rescue a candidate already more than sixfold short of
the binding threshold. They remain RED so they cannot be reused later as
silent positive evidence.

## Independent feasibility close

A fresh Sol feasibility pass challenged whether any untested, legal
scannerless two-consumer mechanism remained after the P1/P2/P3 kills. It found
none.

The decisive lower bound is not based on extrapolating one microbenchmark:

- the best P3 point is `1.5238x`; reaching `10x` requires another `6.56x`,
  reducing candidate time from `5,301 ns` to at most `808 ns`;
- the worst point is `1.0656x`; it requires another `9.38x`, reducing
  `133,552 ns` to `14,231 ns`;
- every measured whole-product point would have to remove `84.76%` to `89.34%`
  of the candidate's remaining time;
- conservative call-tree attribution of the sealed CPU profile assigns
  `1,157,659 us` to direct candidate runtime, `144,501 us` to candidate native
  RegExp, and `167,382 us` to required candidate product construction;
- the comparable M2-attributed total is `1,870,633 us`, making the complete
  `10x` budget about `187,063 us`;
- native matching plus required product construction alone is `311,883 us`,
  or `1.67x` the entire budget, before rollback, traversal, recovery,
  immutable result envelopes, or unowned GC.

The remaining common costs are frozen semantics rather than removable parser
apparatus: UTF-16 source matching, heterogeneous combinator traversal, exact
AST/array/span construction, accepted-M2 recovery provenance, recursion depth
and typed nesting faults, immutable consumer result projection, and the
allocation required to return those products. The live `jsonParser` already
uses first-character dispatch and is a stronger control than P3's synthetic
M2-primitive JSON-shaped grammar. Value's consumer requires recursive typed
results and immutable diagnostics; it cannot supply a cheaper recognizer-only
denominator.

Therefore the parser lane is **terminally infeasible under the current
performance law**, rather than active work awaiting another closure variant.
This does not change the user-owned law and grants no admission or execution
credit.

## Reopening condition

Do not dispatch another closure, table, compiler, scanner, terminal-routing,
source-leaf, or unordered variant. Reopen parser research only when one
concrete mechanism satisfies all of the following before a full campaign:

1. it is mechanistically distinct from every P1/P2/P3 killed family;
2. it remains scannerless and exposes no token plane, generated grammar,
   fallback runtime, compatibility path, or consumer-specialized engine;
3. the same generic primitive is required by exact Value and `jsonParser`
   products;
4. a small-scale equal-product preflight, including immutable result, already
   clears `>=10x` before bootstrap;
5. it preserves the KEEP laws above and removes displaced machinery with a net
   KISS deletion;
6. it supplies an explicit prediction for where the missing `6.56x` to `9.38x`
   whole-product gain comes from.

Absent such a mechanism, the honest state is terminal feasibility RED.
Re-running killed families, relaxing the denominator, comparing a recognizer
to a full result, or waiving the threshold is forbidden.

The smallest exact policy decision capable of reopening the lane would be an
explicit user amendment of invariant 9 from `CI-low >=10x at every unchanged
whole-product plane` to `CI-low >=1.0x at every unchanged whole-product plane,
plus net deletion`. No agent may infer or enact that amendment. Until the user
changes the law, root records the lane as infeasible and keeps every downstream
