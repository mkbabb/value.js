# Constellation DAG Sol adjudication

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Mode:** tranche development only  
**Verdict:** **REJECT current DAG as formation-close authority; AMEND**

## Evidence

Two independent hostile Sol reads and one fresh Sol adjudication reproduced
the following defects against the repository packets rather than the root
status prose:

1. formation nodes could satisfy root close without an ordered full-subject
   `P1 -> P2 -> P3 -> Clean A -> Clean B -> admission` chain, immutable
   predecessor evidence, complete family coverage, or verified hashes;
2. parser formation was incorrectly coupled to the future candidate-consumer,
   deletion, release and released-rebind execution chain;
3. Value's candidate graph omitted `L2 -> L5`, `A3 -> U4` and `U3 -> H1`,
   while Keyframes W2 received only L1/L5 instead of L1-L5;
4. Fourier's exact `W2.U4 -> W1.U5` and `W11.U2 -> W6` milestones were
   flattened into inaccurate whole-wave edges;
5. Keyframes W3's tier pack and W10's integrated post-W4-W9 pack were treated
   as one artifact;
6. receipt issuer, subject and recipient semantics were ambiguous, and the
   Atlas receipt was unconditional despite its graph-dependent contract;
7. Glass and Fourier tuple nodes carried version assertions rather than exact
   tag/commit/tarball/closure identities;
8. the BBNF prerequisite used the stale name "Value W3" instead of the current
   `V.L6.css-path-abi-freeze`; and
9. Value's packet counted three unrelated specialized slices
   (GenericActionBar, CSS/path, API) as successive full-subject passes.

The ninth defect reopens Value formation admission. The immutable packet
`aa6840601388258246677c1c619deeadd763d5dbddb56f9feb92b2b803961840`
remains a strong formation candidate, and the earlier clean-audit artifacts
remain evidence. They do not prove the missing prerequisite.

## Binding amendments

- Every repository formation closes through a successive full-subject
  P1/P2/P3 chain. Each pass binds the predecessor evidence hash and the entire
  family coverage manifest. Later passes may reuse immutable evidence and
  prototype only changed or open families, but every family receives one of
  `KEEP`, `FOLD`, `MOVE`, `SPLIT`, or `PRUNE`.
- Clean A reads the post-P3 corpus. Clean B is later and binds the
  post-Clean-A corpus. A missing, mismatched, stale or rejected receipt cannot
  satisfy the chain.
- Parser formation adds the isolated equal-product, every-scale/result-plane
  >=10x CI-low prototype proof before its formation clean pair. The current
  proof remains RED. Candidate consumption, consumer deletion/equivalence,
  release and rebind remain a separate future execution chain.
- P-B1 receipt
  `0609951ac1497fa00be9b7e3b7faba4b4a0895319da8d306eaf2efa5ead8eb34`
  is retained only as formation-order and authority acknowledgment evidence.
- Value adds `L1.candidate -> L2.candidate -> L5.candidate`,
  `A3 -> U4`, `U3 -> H1`, and full L1-L5 ownership into Keyframes W2.
  `A3 -> H1` is removed.
- Fourier restores `W2.U4 -> W1.U5 -> W1.close` and
  `W11.U2 -> W6`; whole `W11 -> W6` is removed.
- Keyframes records distinct W3 tier and W10 integrated packs. W10 issues
  distinct demo, Glass, Atlas and Slides crater results. Atlas receives either
  a bounded conflict delta or exact graph-backed `NO_CONFLICT`, never an
  unconditional status receipt.
- BBNF waits on both the parse-that released-coordinate Value rebind and
  `V.L6.css-path-abi-freeze`.
- Artifact records are fail-closed until version, source commit, tag name and
  object, peeled commit, tarball, pack, closure and peer-map identities are
  complete. The Fourier tuple also binds three exact member artifact IDs and a
  tuple-manifest hash.

## Required graph falsifiers

The amended graph must reject:

1. an orphan or hashless satisfied formation node;
2. a P2 predecessor hash or coverage manifest that does not bind P1;
3. Clean B predating Clean A absorption or reading another corpus;
4. parser formation fed by future execution proof;
5. P-B1 reused as candidate-consumption or release credit;
6. deletion of any corrected Value or exact Fourier unit edge;
7. reuse of the W3 pack identity as the W10 integrated pack;
8. swapped receipt issuer/recipient or subject identity;
9. version-only producer or unresolved Fourier tuple member;
10. stale "Value W3" BBNF order;
11. an affected Atlas edge with neither a crater nor bounded conflict receipt;
12. root close while any repository admission remains unsatisfied; and
13. rejected or superseded evidence used as positive satisfaction.

## Terminal disposition

**FOLD** the useful wave graph into schema v2. **PRUNE** every false-close edge
and ambiguous identity. **KEEP** the repository wave packets and immutable
evidence. **MOVE** future parser release work out of formation. **SPLIT**
Keyframes pack identities and conditional consumer receipts.

No product source or peer repository was modified by this adjudication.
