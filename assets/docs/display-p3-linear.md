### Where it came from

It's Display P3's own linear light, the values its sRGB-shaped curve encodes. CSS Color 4 names it `display-p3-linear`.

### How to read it

Channels proportional to light on P3's wider primaries; 0.5 is half the light of a full channel.

### What to watch for

- As with linear sRGB, blend here and choose elsewhere, and keep the numbers in floats; at 8 bits the darks band.
