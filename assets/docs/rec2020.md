### Where it came from

The ITU published Recommendation BT.2020 in 2012 for ultra-high-definition television. Its primaries sit on the spectral locus, monochromatic light at 630, 532 and 467 nm, so it encloses nearly everything a screen can show today.

### How to read it

Channels from 0 to 1 on very wide primaries. value.js decodes them with a plain 2.4 power, the BT.1886 curve of a reference display, rather than the camera curve BT.2020 itself specifies.

### What to watch for

- No consumer display covers it all, so a Rec. 2020 colour near its corners must be mapped for any screen you own.
- HDR video often arrives on Rec. 2020 primaries with a PQ or HLG curve instead; that's ICtCp's world, not this encoding's.
